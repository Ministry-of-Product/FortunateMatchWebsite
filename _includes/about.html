What is FortunateMatch?

FortunateMatch drives connections through event-specific intent. What are you looking for today? What do you have to offer? These answers guide AI-powered suggestions for the most valuable introductions.

As an event attendee, you get help finding the most relevant people to meet at this specific event — so that chance encounters become meaningful opportunities. As the event continues, notes from participants help surface even more matches. This turns the event into a group effort to help everyone find the connections they are looking for, as quickly as possible.
